01 Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to andrew.andrew-s-group.com and any subdomains operated by Andrew Joshua Adole as a personal portfolio (the "Site"). It does not cover andrew-s-group.com, which is a separate company with its own privacy policy governing client engagements.

02 What data is collected

The Site is designed to collect the minimum data needed to respond to you. Depending on how you interact with it:

FeatureData collectedWhy
Contact links (email / phone)Whatever you choose to include in your own messageTo respond to your inquiry
AI chat widgetMessage text, a randomly generated session ID, and message history for that sessionTo generate a relevant reply and maintain conversational context
Telegram botsYour Telegram messages and public profile info as shared with the botTo process your inquiry and route qualified leads
General browsingStandard technical data (browser type, approximate location from IP, pages visited)Basic site analytics and security

No account creation, passwords, or payment details are collected through this Site.

03 How data is used

  • To respond to messages sent via the contact links, chat widget, or Telegram bots.
  • To generate automated replies in the AI chat assistant based on your message and the ongoing conversation.
  • To improve the reliability of demo automations shown on the Site.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the Site.

Chat and bot messages are not used to build an advertising profile of you, and are not sold to third parties.

04 Third-party services & processors

To provide the automated features on this Site, messages you send are processed by the following categories of third-party infrastructure:

Webhook / automation platform (n8n)
Telegram (for bot-based projects)
CRM / database storage (e.g. Airtable)
Language model providers

Each of these processes data under its own terms and security practices. Where a bot is hosted on Telegram, Telegram's own privacy policy also applies to your use of that platform.

05 Cookies & local storage

The Site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The AI chat widget keeps a temporary session identifier in memory for the duration of your visit so it can maintain conversation context; this identifier is not linked to your identity and is not persisted after you close the tab, beyond what is retained by the automation backend as described above.

06 Data retention

Messages sent through the contact form or chat widget are retained only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for a reasonable follow-up period afterward. Telegram bot conversations are retained according to the storage used by that specific automation (typically a CRM record for qualified leads), and can be deleted on request.

07 Data sharing & disclosure

Data is not sold. It may be shared only with the third-party service providers listed above, strictly to operate the features you use, or where disclosure is required by law.

08 Your rights

You can request to:

  • Know what data has been collected about you through the Site's chat or bot features.
  • Request correction or deletion of that data.
  • Opt out of future contact.

To exercise any of these, reach out using the contact details below.

09 Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the Site's features change. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
